Delliquadri Residence

A single-story ranch house in Kingston, NY receives a new addition and interior renovation. The project enlarges an existing rear addition, carefully pulling back its southeast corner to maintain a deliberate offset from the original brick wall. The cathedral ceiling of the previous addition is replaced with a flat ceiling and high-performance roof assembly, improving both energy efficiency and headroom clarity.

The new addition is clad in LP SmartSide smooth clapboard and supported on helical piles — avoiding excavation while allowing precise positioning relative to the existing structure. A continuous layer of rigid exterior insulation, closed-cell spray foam at the floor, and loose-fill insulation above the ceiling bring the envelope well beyond code minimum performance. Interior work includes new flooring, tile, paint, lighting, and door selections throughout.
